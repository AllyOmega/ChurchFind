      About the data

      Where it comes from

      Every record is scraped from OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API — specifically, every feature tagged amenity=place_of_worship with religion=christian inside a US state boundary. Nothing is hand-entered, and nothing is taken from a site that forbids scraping.

      What it is not

      OpenStreetMap is volunteer-maintained, so coverage is uneven: a rural county may be thinner than a city, denominations are often blank, and a congregation that has moved or closed can linger. Treat this as a starting point and call ahead. Found something wrong? Fix it at the source and it will flow into the next scrape.
